FinTech Plus announced a successful Q2 2023 with notable financial results, including a 25% year-over-year revenue increase to $125 million and a 30% EBITDA margin. The gross profit margin is at 58%, attributed to cost efficiencies. Net income rose to $16 million from $10 million in Q2 2022. The company expanded its total addressable market through new product lines, such as high-yield savings and a RoboAdvisor platform, and diversified its asset-backed securities portfolio.
